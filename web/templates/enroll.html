Tips for best clone quality ⭐
  • Mic ~20–25 cm (about a hand-span) from your mouth — closer makes plosives pop, farther sounds hollow
  • Quiet room — close windows, kill fans, no music
  • Use your conversational voice, not "narrator" voice
  • No long pauses, no "umm/uhh", no laughs (VAD trims silence but it's better to just keep going)
  • If you stumble, keep going — we crop to the middle 8 s automatically
