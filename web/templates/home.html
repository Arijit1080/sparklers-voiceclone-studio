Quick start

  1. Plug in the USB audio codec (mic + speaker on the same device).
  2. Go to Enroll, type a name, hit start, wait for the high beep, then speak continuously for 30 seconds.
  3. Go to Speak, pick the voice, type any English sentence, hit synth → audio plays through the speaker.
  4. Check Dashboard for live tegrastats while you train and synth.
